# Code of Conduct

Harpia adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and focus
criticism on ideas and code, never on people. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated, in project spaces or
when representing the project elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially; maintainers may warn,
temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, following the Covenant's enforcement
guidelines.
